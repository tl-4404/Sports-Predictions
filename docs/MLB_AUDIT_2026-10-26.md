# MLB pipeline audit — October 26, 2026

## Verdict and scope

The pipeline had material evaluation, timing, grading and publication defects. These
are reproducible code findings, not proof of which individual losses they caused.
The production database and a complete recent decision/entry ledger were unavailable;
recent real-money accuracy, ROI and drawdown remain unknown. No before/after daily
prop accuracy improvement can honestly be claimed yet.

This branch implements model `mlb-audit-v5` and chat policy `chat-audited-v5.0`, fixes
those defects, and adds a transparent statistical challenger. The challenger improves
on a simple annual-rate baseline in a held-out season and remains shadow. The chat
judge remains the decision lane, with data eligibility enforced in code.

The audit covered `tl-4404/Betting-Aid-App` at main commit
`e61920059f08b450add6dcb39ec9b599dfad0cf6`: serving, simulation, research, judging,
pricing, calibration, grading, historical dataset construction, training preprocessing,
app surfacing, tests and documentation. Unrelated NBA code was not changed.

Implemented MLB targets are pitcher Ks and outs. Fantasy score is discussed in
prompts but has no implemented joint model. MLB moneylines, totals, team totals and
hitter props have no verified implemented forecasting pipeline here. Polymarket
order books, fees, executable prices and actual fills are also not implemented.

No `sportsaid.db` was present. Seven August 8 ledger JSON documents are forecasts,
research and reruns, not a complete recent graded ledger. Prior chat recommendations
cannot be assumed to be outputs of repository code that actually ran. This branch is
a reviewable code change, not a deployment.

## Findings and changes

|---|---|---|

| P0 | Live/backtest CLV compared our probability with the close. | Closing fair probability minus entry fair probability for overs, opposite sign for unders. This measures market movement independently of our forecast. |
| P0 | Grading could use incomplete games, join by name across games, and permanently void API failures. | Require a final game and complete two-starter box; use game/player IDs. Failed/nonfinal/incomplete boxes remain pending. A confirmed nonstarter in a complete final box can be voided. |
| P0 | PrizePicks ROI used an invented standalone price derived from parlay break-even. | Remove per-leg ROI/PnL and automatic entry EV/playability. Add pure entry-EV math for supplied actual joint outcomes and settlement terms. |
| P1 | Pick'em dropped push mass when calculating entry win probabilities. | Store push probability and unconditional More/Less probabilities separately from the conditional no-push probability. |

| P1 | Tiny probability buckets could be enabled by a larger global sample; mappings could reverse probability order. | Require 200 segment observations, two usable knots and 30 observations per knot. ### Accounting details

Closing prices must match game/player/market/threshold and come from the last
complete live-source book snapshot strictly before scheduled start, within 15
minutes of start. Moved/disappeared thresholds and missing/late closes yield `None`.
This is fair-probability-point CLV, not ROI or execution quality. Actual delayed
starts and book-specific settlement still require richer event-state data.

An undated CSV cannot establish betting ROI. Historical betting evaluation now needs
game/player IDs, observed/decision/start timestamps, archived pregame lineup
provenance and features available by decision time. Missing/late closing timestamps
cannot establish CLV. Actual box-score lineups are explicitly retrospective skill
diagnostics even when a price file is attached. Synthetic results are labeled.

Previously saved wrong CLV/void records remain unchanged. Reconcile them separately
against archived evidence before making a new performance claim. Versioned reporting
excludes legacy model versions rather than silently recasting their results.

## Verifiable model inspiration

These documented approaches are useful references. Fantasy projection rankings do
not establish profitable daily prop betting.

| Primary source | Documented method | Application here |
|---|---|---|
| [Tom Tango: Marcel](https://tangotiger.net/marcel/) | Multiple years, recency weights, regression, aging and reliability. | Multi-year shrunk skill challenger. This variant lacks an aging curve and is not a full Marcel reproduction. |
| [Christopher Fonnesbeck: Bayesian Marcel](https://www.pymc-labs.com/blog-posts/bayesian-marcel) | Estimate weighting/aging effects and express projection uncertainty. | Tune recency/regression on past seasons; retain conditional Beta uncertainty. This is not the article's hierarchical PyMC implementation, and intervals omit future talent drift. |
| [Derek Carty: THE BAT X for pitchers, February 19, 2026](https://blogs.fangraphs.com/introducing-the-bat-x-for-pitchers-and-the-batcast-stuff-model/) | Blend base projections with pitch-level stuff, separate event models and pitch/handedness interactions. | Separate K, BB+HBP and hit fitting. Remove unvalidated shortcut adjustments and inaccurate branding; a fitted stuff model remains future work. |
| [Ariel Cohen: ATC](https://fantasy.fangraphs.com/the-atc-projection-system/) and [2020 methodology](https://fantasy.fangraphs.com/the-2020-atc-projections/) | Historical-performance/stat-specific aggregation, with rates separate from playing time. | Preserve skill/workload separation. No proprietary coefficients, paid forecast histories or inaccessible consensus
projections were invented. External models remain challenge/benchmark inputs.

## Empirical test: frozen 2025 annual event rates

`mlb/skill_baseline.py` forecasts annual pitcher rates from the preceding three
seasons, weighted 1, `decay`, `decay²`. The league prior uses only the preceding
season. Stat-specific pseudo-samples shrink estimates toward that prior.

Select hyperparameters by BF-weighted binomial log loss on target years 2023/2024;
freeze before testing 2025. Candidate decays: 0, .25, .5, .75, 1. Prior BF: 25, 50,
100, 200, 400, 800. Compare against previous-season-only rates with a 50-BF prior.
That baseline resembles the old single-season shrinkage style, not the entire daily
pipeline, which used in-season data and other assumptions.

Held-out cohort: 321 pitchers with at least one start and 100 BF in 2025, including
rookies, totaling 127,286 BF. Future exposure defines evaluation cohort/weights only,
not forecasting inputs. Six MLB StatsAPI season payloads supply the data; source
hashes and frozen aggregate counts are checked in. No betting lines or returns enter.

| Annual rate target | Baseline weighted RMSE (percentage points) | Challenger weighted RMSE (percentage points) | Relative reduction | Selected decay / prior BF |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| K/BF | 3.916 | 3.672 | 6.23% | .50 / 200 |
| BB+HBP/BF | 2.191 | 1.920 | 12.36% | .50 / 200 |
| Hits/BF | 3.024 | 2.602 | 13.94% | .75 / 800 |

Mean per-BF log loss improved for all targets. Two thousand paired pitcher-block
bootstrap resamples give these 95% intervals for challenger minus baseline loss:

- K: `[-0.0008882, -0.0001330]`.
- BB+HBP: `[-0.0009623, -0.0004298]`.
- Hits: `[-0.0009824, -0.0004068]`.

The direction also held in diagnostic slices of at least 10 starts (184 pitchers),
at least 300 BF (175), and less than 200 prior BF across three seasons (97). Slices
reuse the same holdout and are not independent validation seasons. Intervals
condition on the selected tuning procedure. Event Brier gains are modest. This test
does not validate daily prop probabilities, workload tails or positive betting EV.

Full output: [validation/skill_holdout_2025.json](validation/skill_holdout_2025.json).
Reproduce offline after installing `requirements-dev.txt` with `make audit-baseline`.

## Validation

All seven legacy script suites pass in separate processes. Those scripts mutate
global imports and should not be collected together in pytest. Forty-five focused
pytest cases pass, covering chat execution and v5 behavior. `git diff --check` and
Python compilation checks are clean. The annual skill benchmark reproduces from
the frozen MLB aggregate snapshot.

The aggregate snapshot was retrieved in 2026, not archived at each historical
decision time. Official statistical revisions and the future-exposure evaluation
cohort are further reasons to treat it as an annual skill diagnostic. The selected
hit prior is at the search grid's upper boundary; future grid expansion needs a new
unseen evaluation period rather than another pass over this holdout.

Regressions cover stale/future/fixture prices, incomplete lineups, unknown role,
offered-price EV, UI expiry and raw LLM rejection; immutable ledgers; target/future
game exclusion; doubleheader identity and failed-box retry; exact pregame closes;
first raw calibration records, monotonicity and future fits; low caps and numerical
+4 BF; unconditional push math and actual-entry settlement; chronological preprocessing
and both starters; dated factors and reviewed role evidence.

Heavy torch retraining, learned Statcast stuff fitting, live authenticated LLM runs,
paid odds feeds and money execution were not run. Unit and synthetic test success
does not establish profitable betting.

## Operational requirements

The numerical challenger stays shadow. The judge needs reviewed current usage
evidence through `MLB_USAGE_EVIDENCE_PATH` or `mlb/cache/usage_evidence.json` before
publication. Store an array of records with matching slate/game/player IDs,
observation/review timestamps, `reviewed: true`, expected role (`starter`, `opener`
or `bulk`), an HTTPS source URL, specific evidence notes, and optional integer
`pitch_cap`. Observations must be within 24 hours and precede the game.

The review flag is a trusted upstream researcher/reviewer assertion. The adapter
checks identity, timing and structure, not the article's authenticity or truth. Keep
this input separate from unrestricted LLM prose and retain provenance. Without a
connected reviewed evidence feed, new official recommendations intentionally remain
HOLD. This coverage change must be considered in future comparisons.

Fresh live prices, nine unique opposing batters, future game start and positive EV
at the offered price are also required. A higher probability cannot override missing
evidence. PrizePicks remains leg research until exact entry partners, joint forecasts,
stakes, payout and push terms are supplied and recorded.

game/player IDs, raw/final probabilities, input snapshots, versions, research/policy
mode, timestamps, prices/payouts, grades, actual entries/stakes/fills, settlement and

Report Brier/log loss, calibration by market, coverage, CLV and returns after actual
costs. Resample by game/day or pitcher, not correlated alternate lines. Separate
tuning/calibration/final test periods and track price, role and late-lineup failures.

Still needed: historical current-year platoon snapshots; aging/minor-league
translation; a fitted pitch-mix/stuff model; talent-drift uncertainty; learned
workload/manager hooks; bullpen/rest/weather inputs; joint dependence; and real
platform execution/settlement. Existing 70/30 blending, ±5-point judge band, 64% cap
and 6/8-point hurdles remain unvalidated policy defaults, not proof of edge.

Park/umpire ratios remain confounded by the players and games in their samples.
Dated provenance prevents time leakage; it does not validate their adjustment size.
Context-neutralized, out-of-sample estimates are still needed.

Official box labels are preferable to event-reconstructed training outs. Historical
void reconciliation, actual delayed starts and atomic empty-board/failure snapshots
also need production evidence/handling. No simulation size or probability cap can
substitute for a clean prospective evaluation.
